## 🌍 About E-Kabadi

**E-Kabadi** is a smart scrap management platform that connects **citizens and scrap collectors** through an intelligent digital ecosystem.

The platform uses **AI-assisted scrap identification, smart pricing, location-based pickup requests, digital payments, and reward mechanisms** to make scrap collection more convenient, transparent, and rewarding.

Instead of the traditional:

> **Find Kabadi → Negotiate → Sell → Get Paid**

E-Kabadi provides:

> **Capture → Identify → Estimate → Request → Collect → Pay → Reward ♻️**

---

# 🚨 The Problem

Traditional scrap collection is often:

- ❌ Unorganized
- ❌ Time-consuming
- ❌ Dependent on manual price estimation
- ❌ Difficult to track
- ❌ Lacking transaction transparency
- ❌ Not sufficiently incentivized
- ❌ Inefficient for both citizens and collectors

At the same time, recyclable materials that have economic value are often mixed with general waste.

### 💡 Our Solution

E-Kabadi creates a digital bridge between **waste generators and scrap collectors**.

The platform enables citizens to:

📸 Identify their scrap  
💰 Estimate its value  
📍 Find collectors  
🚛 Schedule collection  
💳 Receive digital payment  
🎁 Earn rewards  

While collectors get:

📦 Pickup requests  
🤖 AI-assisted identification  
📍 Location-based navigation  
💰 Transaction management  
🪙 Eco Coins  

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 👤 Citizen Mode

### 📸 AI Scrap Identification

Citizens can capture or upload an image of their scrap.

The AI-assisted system identifies the material category and helps determine its approximate value.

---

### 💰 Smart Price Estimation

The system provides an estimated scrap value based on the identified material and pricing logic.

This helps create a more transparent selling experience.

---

### 📍 Nearby Collector Discovery

Citizens can find available collectors and request a pickup.

---

### 🗺️ Pickup Tracking

Location-based functionality helps track the scrap collection process.

---

### 💳 Digital Payment

After successful collection, citizens receive digital payment for their scrap.

---
